# HelQix Headquarters — night festival scene

An interactive 3D model built from the HelQix concept render. It shows a curved glass atrium under a sweeping LED ribbon, two wings with rooftop rotundas, and illuminated HelQix signage, including the services panel (AI & LLM Systems, Quantum Computing, Aerospace & Defense, Simulation & Digital Twin, Robotics & Automation, Cloud & Edge Infrastructure).

The campus around it has:
- a wet reflective plaza
- cherry blossoms with drifting petals
- fountains, flags and the monument sign
- a rotating armillary sphere sculpture
- sweeping sky beams, drones, fireworks and a lit city skyline

Open `index.html` in a recent desktop browser, or serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server`).

The control bar has four camera views (Entrance, Campus, Aerial, Sculpture) and four toggles:
- **Festival lights**: rainbow chase on every LED strip
- **Fireworks**
- **Sky beams**
- **Slow orbit**

All textures and signage are drawn in code. Only three.js r160 is loaded, from jsDelivr.
